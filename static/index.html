🔴 Live scan LIVE

Open the group in WhatsApp Web (or any chat window), press Start live scan and choose that window or tab. Every few seconds Sloth Digest looks at it, reads the new messages and keeps the digest up to date. Nothing to upload.

  1. Press Start live scan and pick the chat window or browser tab.
  2. Keep the chat visible and scroll as you read. Zoom the chat in a little for better reading.
  3. Press Stop and save when you are done. The final digest goes to History.

Preview stays in your browser. Pictures sent to the local server are read and dropped, never saved.

Honest limits: this is near-live, not instant (a local 4B model needs several seconds per picture). It only sees what is on screen, so scroll up to cover older messages. On a Mac, allow Screen Recording for your browser in System Settings, Privacy and Security. Reading small text from pictures can have mistakes, and the receipts show exactly what was read.
